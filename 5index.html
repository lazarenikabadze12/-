Japan

Japan is an island nation in East Asia, known for its unique blend of ancient traditions and cutting-edge modernity. Surrounded by the Pacific Ocean, it stretches in an arc from the Sea of Okhotsk to the East China Sea, featuring a diverse landscape dominated by mountains and forests. As a constitutional monarchy with a rich cultural heritage, Japan has made significant global contributions in technology, science, and popular culture, while maintaining a distinct identity shaped by centuries of history. Tokyo, the capital, serves as a dynamic center of innovation and urban life.

USA

The United States is a federal republic composed of 50 states and a capital district, Washington, D.C., with a diverse geography that spans from the Atlantic to the Pacific and includes Alaska in the northwest and Hawaii in the Pacific Ocean. Founded on principles of democracy and federalism, it has a presidential system of government and a rich history shaped by Indigenous civilizations, European colonization, revolution, expansion, and civil war. A major global power, the U.S. exerts significant influence in political, economic, cultural, and military affairs worldwide.

France

France,[h] officially the French Republic,[i] is a country primarily located in Western Europe.[j] Its overseas regions and territories include French Guiana in South America, Saint Pierre and Miquelon in the North Atlantic, the French West Indies, and many islands in Oceania and the Indian Ocean. Metropolitan France shares borders with Belgium and Luxembourg to the north; Germany to the northeast; Switzerland to the east; Italy and Monaco to the southeast; Andorra and Spain to the south; and a maritime border with the United Kingdom to the northwest. Its metropolitan area extends from the Rhine to the Atlantic Ocean and from the Mediterranean Sea to the English Channel and the North Sea. Its 18 integral regions—five of which are overseas—span a combined area of 632,702 km2 (244,288 sq mi), with a total population estimated at over 69.1 million in 2026. Its capital, largest city and main cultural and economic centre is Paris.

Brasil

Bounded by the Atlantic Ocean on the east, Brazil has a coastline of 7,491 kilometers (4,655 mi),[15] among the longest in the world. Covering roughly half of South America's land area, it borders all other countries and territories on the continent except Ecuador and Chile.[16] Brazil encompasses a wide range of tropical and subtropical landscapes, as well as wetlands, savannas, plateaus, and low mountains. It contains most of the Amazon basin, including the world's largest river system and most extensive virgin tropical forest. Brazil has diverse wildlife, a variety of ecological systems, and extensive natural resources spanning numerous protected habitats.[15] It ranks first among 17 megadiverse countries, with its natural heritage being the subject of significant global interest, as environmental degradation (through processes such as deforestation) directly affect global issues such as climate change and biodiversity loss.
